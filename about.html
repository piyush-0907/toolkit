About Toolkit

Free, simple tools for everyday tasks, all in one place.

What we're building

Toolkit brings together 50 online tools for PDFs, images, QR codes, calculations, text, development and design. Each tool does one job, opens instantly and asks for nothing before you start.

What we care about

  • Simple. One task per page, clear controls, no clutter.
  • Private. Tools are designed to run in your browser so your content can stay on your device.
  • Accessible. Keyboard-friendly, readable in light and dark, and usable on a phone.
  • Honest. We say plainly what a tool does and what it doesn't.

Where things stand

This site is a preview of the interface. The tools are not connected yet, and each page says so.
